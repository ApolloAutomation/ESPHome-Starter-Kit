# Contributing

Thanks for your interest in the ESK-1 Starter Kit. This is a DIY tinkerer board, and we'd love to see what you build with it.

## Case and accessory designs

The Starter Kit ships without an official enclosure on purpose, so you can design your own. If you make a case, mount, or other 3D-printable accessory, we'd love to include it.

1. Design against the STEP placeholder models in [`hardware/cad/pcb-placeholders/`](hardware/cad/pcb-placeholders/).
2. Fork this repo and add your part under `hardware/3d-prints/` (STL) alongside its source (STEP/F3D where possible).
3. Open a pull request describing the part, which board(s) it fits, and any print notes (material, supports, orientation).

Photos of the printed result in the PR are always appreciated.

## Issues

Found a problem with the docs or the ESPHome config? Open an issue and include your board revision and ESPHome version.

## Questions

For support, feedback, and general discussion, join the [Apollo Automation Discord](http://dsc.gg/ApolloAutomation).
